Convert

Turns a raster image into clean vector shapes. Drag Look from Clean to Crunchy, or set each control yourself. Upscale goes from 0.25× (shrinks first, chunkier) up to 6×. Pixel keeps the stair-steps of low-resolution images.

Batch convert

In Convert, open the Batch view, then drop in many images or use Add images / Add folder. Choose an output folder on this computer and press Convert and save to folder: every SVG is saved in that one folder with the original name (a number is added if a name is taken, and nothing already there is replaced). Same for all uses the Convert settings for every image; Different per image lets you click an image, see it live, and give it its own settings. A ✎ tag marks images with their own settings. You can also download everything as a .zip.

Compose

Build a page from your own graphics. Everything you convert in the Convert tab is saved automatically in one folder (BootTool/Library in your home folder). Hover the grey Library tab at the bottom of Compose and click a graphic, or drag it onto the page. + Add file brings in any SVG or picture from your computer.

The Artboard can be any size from 20 to 1200 mm: pick A5, A4 or A3 with Landscape or Portrait, or type your own width and height. Select moves, resizes (corner handles keep proportions, Shift frees them) and rotates (the round handle on top). The side panel has exact position and size, flip, centre, order and the layer list (hide, lock, rename). Colour: click a colour chip, then a swatch to replace that colour everywhere in the selection, or tick "whole page". Text uses any installed font (more fonts: drop .ttf or .otf files in BootTool/Fonts) and becomes real vector outlines.

Nodes edits individual points and curve handles. Pen draws new shapes like Illustrator's: click for corners, click and drag for curves, click the first point to close. Combine shapes: Unite, Subtract, Intersect, Exclude, Split, Combine, Separate, Join ends. Undo and Redo go back 30 steps. Shortcuts: V select, A nodes, P pen, T text, S shape, Ctrl/Cmd+Z, +D duplicate, Delete, arrow keys to nudge, Space+drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

Group (Ctrl/Cmd+G) keeps selected graphics together without merging them; open the ▾ arrow in Layers to see inside, Ungroup is Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+G. Fill (F): click inside a hollow area and a filled shape appears directly behind the graphic, as its own layer. Hold Shift to group it with the graphic, click a fill again to change its colour, Grow tucks it under the lines and Close gaps treats tiny breaks in an outline as closed. If the area is open to the outside, close it with the Pen first. Send to Print flattens the page and opens it in Print as a Spot colour, Flat graphic job at its real size, with one ink for each colour you used. Save as SVG saves the page as one SVG.

Print

Makes screen-print plates on a real page. Pick a Method: One ink (a single plate), CMYK (four plates) or Spot colour (up to 10 inks of your choice, paper never printed). For spot colour choose Flat graphic (solid plates with trap) or Photo (every ink is halftoned on its own plate, with soft colour matching so gradients are shared between neighbouring inks).

Levels works like Photoshop's: the left handle sets what prints as solid ink, the right handle sets what drops out to paper, and the middle handle lightens or darkens the midtones. Move the handles in to remove faint detail. Auto sets them from the picture itself.

Screen halftone follows Photoshop's Image → Mode → Bitmap → Halftone Screen: set Frequency (LPI), Angle, Shape and Output resolution. Dots merge inside shapes and are cut off at their edges. Dither makes a random-looking grain instead of dots: Floyd–Steinberg, Atkinson, Stucki, Bayer, Blue noise, Serpentine (long maze-like strokes, like Turing patterns; pick Mazes, Coral, Fingerprints, Loops or Spots) or Random. Dither size sets how big each grain is on the page, and for Serpentine Pattern size sets how wide the strokes are. CMYK colours and screen angles can be edited; separation always uses standard CMYK.

Design size: Fit to page scales the design to the page (inside the artwork margin). Set size prints it at exactly the width or height you type, in mm, centred, with proportions kept; if it is bigger than the page, the page becomes a Custom size that fits. Save to Print library keeps the finished design (its real size and ink colours) so Simulate can use it.

Simulate

Shows your designs on a T-shirt drawn to real size. Choose the size (XS to XXL, unisex chart), short or long sleeve, front or back, and the shirt colour (or Use Print colour). Hover the Libraries tab at the bottom and click a design from the Convert or Print library to put it on the shirt; add up to 10 per side. Drag to move (it snaps to the centre line), drag a corner to resize, or type Width, Height, From collar and From centre in cm. Centre, Chest and Full front are quick placements. You can also drag a design from the library onto the shirt. The round handle above a selected design rotates it (hold Shift to snap to 15°), or type an angle. Crop designs to the shirt (on by default) hides anything that hangs past the shirt, like a clipping mask. Arrow keys nudge by 1 mm (Shift: 1 cm), Delete removes. Save mock-up as JPG saves the picture; tick Include measurements to add the size lines to it.

  • Downloaded plates are always pure black, with registration marks and the ink name on every plate.
  • The ink name is live text in Arial Narrow. In Illustrator use Type → Create Outlines if you need it as shapes.
  • Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
